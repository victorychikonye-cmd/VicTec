About the company

Introducing VicTec

VicTec is a Zimbabwe-focused education technology initiative developing AI-powered assistants for secondary school learning. Our goal is to make personalised explanations, structured practice and revision support more accessible to students preparing for ZIMSEC examinations.

Students will be able to access VicTec through the website and WhatsApp, with learning support designed for both O Level and A Level.

✓Designed around Zimbabwean learners and ZIMSEC study needs.
✓Focused on understanding concepts, not just memorising answers.
✓Built to make digital learning simple and accessible.
Who we support

O Level and A Level learning

VicTec is being developed to support learners across both ZIMSEC secondary school pathways.
